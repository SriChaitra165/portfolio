About Me

I'm a Computer Science Engineering student who is curious about how things work and even more curious about how they can be made better. I chose CSE because I've always enjoyed coding, but what truly fascinated me was the idea of building systems and communicating with them—and I wanted to experience that process for myself.

I enjoy solving problems, turning ideas into working applications, and continuously learning along the way. I'm particularly interested in building full-stack applications powered by AI that can address meaningful, real-world problems. I'm still exploring where I want this journey to take me, and I'm happy to let the things I build help me discover that.

Outside of technology, I write poetry and sketch. Writing helps me capture everyday observations, while drawing has been a part of my life since childhood. I believe that this creative side influences the way I approach technology too: I like to observe, question, experiment, and understand before I decide how to solve a problem.

Through projects and hackathons, I hope to keep testing myself, learn from others, and step beyond what I already know.

Writing · Poetry

Memorable

There are countless days
I've been like this.
I don't even remember how many.

After all,
this is just another day in my life.
Yet today, it happens slower and softer.

I don't know what hurdles tomorrow brings,
or what worries from the past haunt me
after this moment ends.

But now,
in this quiet moment of stillness,
the world outside moves messier
than one can ever realize—
all at once,
and once for all.

This moment,
this peace of solitude,
is a window into the world around
and a chamber of our little inner world.

So let me cherish this
a little longer.
